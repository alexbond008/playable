B

The part that makes it different

The Truth Gate.

Before any 3D exists, the agent writes the physics model. That model must reproduce every official answer, to the source's own decimals. Code is compiled and run for real with clang; measured data is fitted. Here is one question, start to finish.

What the game reveals when you solve Q3

  1. Torque needs current  I = T / K = 3 / 0.215 A
  2. Speed  2000 rpm209.4 rad/s
  3. Back-EMF  E = Kω = 0.2 × 209.441.9 V
  4. Armature drop  IR = 15 × 0.11.5 V
  5. Supply  V = E + IR43.4 V

In the game you don't read this first. You predict, turn the supply knob until the motor holds 3 Nm at 2000 rpm, and then time slows and this opens.

C

How a game gets made

Five steps. One of them is a gate.

Everything runs inside your agent, following SKILL.md. You get an ETA up front.

D

Every level runs the same loop

Predict first. Then find out.

  1. Mission

    One concept per level. You're told what to find out.

  2. Predict

    Commit to an answer and say how sure you are: Guessing, Fairly sure or Certain.

  3. Play

    Turn the knob, drag the controls. The physics is the model that passed the gate.

  4. Reveal

    Time slows to ×0.25 and a cutaway opens on what just happened.

  5. Power-up

    The equation unlocks into your Toolbox, live.

  6. Recall

    Questions come back after 1, 3 and 9 days.

Stars: solved prediction right no hint

F

Install

One line, then point it at a PDF.

Works in Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity and OpenCode (Agent Skills format). Nothing to npm install.

STEP 1

Add the skill

npx skills add alexbond008/playable

No npx? Clone it into your skills folder: git clone https://github.com/alexbond008/playable ~/.claude/skills/playable

STEP 2

Ask for a game

› /playable turn my tutorial into a game: ~/uni/tutorial-1.pdf

  • Needs Node.js 20.10+ and Chrome (for the playtest).
  • A lesson takes about 1–2 hours to build; you get an ETA up front.
  • Out comes one HTML file. Playing is instant and works offline.
G

Limits

What it won't do.

  1. It won't do your problems for you.

    It teaches what's in your material and checks it against your answers.

  2. It isn't instant to build.

    A lesson takes about 1–2 hours, with an ETA up front. Playing is instant and offline.

  3. It doesn't cover every subject yet.

    The kit starts with electrical, mechanical, control and embedded systems. New domains grow the kit.

  4. No haptics on iPhone.

    Haptics use navigator.vibrate: Android yes, iOS no.
